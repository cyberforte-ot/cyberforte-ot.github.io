The 10 risks

OWASP says items 1 to 4 are fundamental, 5 to 9 are of similar high importance, and item 10 is not a security issue by itself but would have prevented many incidents.

OT 01 Unknown Assets and Unmanaged External Access

You cannot protect what you do not know you have.

Top 4: fundamental✓ Done OT 02 Devices with Known Vulnerabilities/Issues

A published weakness is an open invitation, especially if it can be reached.

Top 4: fundamental✓ Done OT 03 Inadequate Supplier/Supply Chain Management

Attackers sometimes come in through the people you trust most.

Top 4: fundamental✓ Done OT 04 Loss of Availability

In OT, if it stops (or lies), people can get hurt.

Top 4: fundamental✓ Done OT 05 Insufficient Access Control

Shared passwords on a sticky note are not access control.

Similar high importance✓ Done OT 06 Missing Incident Detection/Reaction Capabilities

The longer an intruder goes unnoticed, the worse it gets.

Similar high importance✓ Done OT 07 Broken Zones and Conduits Design

Split the site into rooms with controlled doorways, not one big open hall.

Similar high importance✓ Done OT 08 Missing Awareness

The best lock is useless if nobody knows it is there, or why to use it.

Similar high importance✓ Done OT 09 Components/Protocols with Insufficient Security Capabilities

Do not build tomorrow's site on yesterday's insecure parts.

Similar high importance✓ Done OT 10 Missing Hardening

Switch off what you do not need, and add a few extra locks.

Would have prevented many incidents✓ Done
