Part 2: OWASP OT Top 10

  • Source: ot.owasp.org and github.com/OWASP/www-project-operational-technology-top-10. Version used: OWASP OT Top 10 2025, repository state of 20 October 2026.
  • Author: the OWASP OT Top 10 project and its contributors.
  • Licence: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0), as stated in the document and its repository.
  • What we changed: we added our own summary, “why it matters”, “how to reduce the risk” and practical checklist to each entry. The original entry is reproduced unchanged, converted from Markdown, with links to other entries pointed at this site or at ot.owasp.org.

Illustrations

The 3D plant, the story drawings and the icons are modelled and drawn in code by CyberForte. They are our own interpretation and are not part of the ENISA or OWASP material. The 3D plant uses the open-source three.js library (MIT licence), included with the site; a flat drawing is shown if your browser cannot run it. The only source image is ENISA's Figure 1, shown unchanged with its credit.

Real-world examples

We only add a real-world example when it is backed by reputable reporting, and we link to the sources. If an investigation is still open, the example is labelled Reported. The examples listed inside the original texts are the authors' own.

Good practice, not a stamp

These guides describe good practice and help with readiness. They are not legal advice, and reading or ticking them does not by itself meet any legal or regulatory requirement.

Your data

This site has no accounts, no tracking and no cookies. Checklist ticks, progress and your light or dark choice are saved only in your own browser (local storage) and never leave your device.
